Privacy Policy & SMS Terms of Service

Privacy Policy

Daiki International Trading Corporation (DITCO) collects only the personal information necessary to conduct business operations with vendors, suppliers, and business partners. This includes name, phone number, and email address.

Information is used solely for operational purposes such as order coordination, scheduling, delivery updates, and business communication.

Mobile opt-in data, SMS consent, and phone numbers collected for SMS communication purposes will not be shared with any third party or affiliates for marketing purposes.

Opt-In Process

SMS consent is collected only through the Daiki International Trading Corporation website contact form at https://www.daikius.com.

Users must complete the form, provide their contact information, and explicitly check the SMS consent checkbox before submitting the form.

Submitting the form with the SMS consent checkbox selected constitutes express consent to receive SMS messages from DITCO at 424-237-1010.

No SMS consent is collected by text message, email, or any other method outside the website form.
