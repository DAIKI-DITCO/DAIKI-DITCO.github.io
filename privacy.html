Privacy Policy & SMS Terms of Service

Privacy Policy

Daiki International Trading Corporation (DITCO) collects only the personal information necessary to conduct business operations with vendors, suppliers, and business partners. This includes name, phone number, and email address.

Information is used solely for operational purposes such as order coordination, scheduling, delivery updates, and business communication.

DITCO does not sell or share mobile opt-in data or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or unrelated purposes.

SMS Opt-In Process

SMS consent is obtained only through the Daiki International Trading Corporation website contact form at https://www.daikius.com.

Users must enter their contact information, check the SMS consent checkbox, and submit the form to opt in.

Submitting the form with the SMS consent checkbox selected constitutes express consent to receive SMS messages from DITCO at 424-237-1010.

No SMS consent is collected by text message, email, or any method outside the website form.
